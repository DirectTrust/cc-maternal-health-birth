## 4.3 Endpoint and Organization Identification

Identify the responsible organization, the deployed endpoint, and its operator. These declarations establish the scope of the capabilities published for that endpoint.

| **Publish**                         | **What to provide**                                                                  |
|-------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Responsible Organization            | Legal or operational organization responsible for the endpoint.                      |
| Endpoint / Direct Address           | Direct address, address pattern, or other endpoint identification used for exchange. |
| Endpoint or Service Name            | Human-readable service name, when used.                                              |
| Endpoint Operator / Intermediary    | HIE, data utility, HISP, or other intermediary when relevant.                        |
| Supported Use Case                  | cc-maternal-health-birth and supported version.                                      |
| Technical / Trading Partner Contact | Onboarding and interoperability contact.                                             |
| Last Updated                        | Date the declarations were last verified.                                            |
